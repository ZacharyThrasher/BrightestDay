## Findings and applied changes (hitscan pass — this run)

_Hand-written summary; re-running the tournament regenerates everything except the findings sections (copy them forward)._ This run: **hitscan build** — hand cannon, super shotgun (per-pellet rays), minigun and railgun are lag-compensated hitscan; the ring blast is 180 m/s with a 0.35 m swept radius; bots aim like people (`bots::AimProfile`: 150–350 ms reaction delay, partial extrapolation, tracking lag that grows with angular speed, gaussian-ish jitter, flick overshoot, real projectile lead and no lead for hitscan). **Before** = this build with the pre-pass gun rows (hand cannon 16, minigun 5, railgun 45). **After** = shipped `assets/tuning/combat.ron`. Same 500 seeds. "Pre-hitscan" = the balance pass 2 run below (slug guns, old bots).

**Applied (damage numbers only, `weapons::gun_spec` + `combat.ron` gun rows): hand cannon 16 → 13, minigun 5 → 4.5, railgun 45 → 40.** Super shotgun unchanged (8 × 9 pellets): it already sits at the gun median.

- **Every gun archetype is now ≤ 2.5× the gun median.** The Gun *form* is still 4.6× the all-form median (3.5× before hitscan): the trims were kept modest on purpose.
- **Why the jump:** the old bots led every shot as if it were a 75 m/s ring blast, so they *missed* moving targets with the railgun (0.20 → 0.54 once they stopped leading hitscan). Bot targets are also slow (median 4–6 m/s, p90 12–14 m/s), which is exactly where hitscan is strongest. Against a pinned 30 m/s strafer the same bots (difficulty 0.7) hit ~7 % of minigun rounds (94 % on a still target); humans swinging on tethers at 20–50 m/s will pull this number down.
- **Global:** KO rate 8.2 → 8.8 /min, credited KO % median 70 → 72, fight time 13.9 s, recovery 78 %, credited KOs 72–73 %: unchanged within noise.
- **Proposed, not applied (lead call, §5i.1 "balance with cost"):** if human telemetry confirms Gun > 2.5× the form median, raise `willpower.form_cost` Gun 2.0 → ~2.6–3.0 rather than cutting damage further (hitscan rounds must keep the DOOM feel). Next damage candidates would be hand cannon 13 → 12 and minigun 4.5 → 4.

## Balance pass 2 (pre-hitscan build, kept for history)

_Hand-written summary; re-running the tournament regenerates everything except these findings sections (copy them forward)._ This run: current build (bots tether only as a fall rescue, commit 146b541). **Before** = pass-1 economy (`target/balance/pass1.ron` = shipped file with `autonomous_pricing: false`); **after** = shipped `assets/tuning/combat.ron`. Same 500 seeds.

**Applied (cost only, lead-approved): autonomous non-creatures priced like creatures.** A Held / Structure / Object recipe that Flies or Walks (“flying hammer”, “wall with legs”) now uses the creature cast base (25) and upkeep (1.5/s): `willpower.autonomous_pricing / autonomous_base / autonomous_upkeep`. Rules:
- The price is judged on the phrase **as typed**, before power and party grafts. Party grafts never raise a price: Wings Day on a hammer still costs a hammer.
- A *typed* graft that makes your own construct hunt pays the difference to a creature base, then creature upkeep. So cast-then-graft is not a discount.
- Integrity is priced from the unadjusted cost. Behaviour is unchanged.

Results:

- Flying hammer detail: 33 k damage and 465 KOs from 994 casts before; the same damage from 880 casts after.
- Global KO rate, KO %, fight time and recovery are unchanged.
- The flying hammer is still the strongest signature. Bots (fixed aim, no dodging) are its best-case victims. Re-check with human telemetry before pricing it further.
- Top-3-mechanic win rates move within noise: Flies 18.5 → 19.8 %, n ≈ 550, ±1.6 %. Win rate responds to *behaviour*, not price, so this is expected.

**Smash targets on the new bots.** The bot fix removed most self-falls:

- **KO %:** still low. The kb sweep in pass 1 showed knockback moves it little (halving growth: +6–14 points), so the smash knobs are untouched. Most KOs are players *falling* off the islands at low %, not being launched.
- **Recovery:** too easy. Side and top blast zones are almost never reached (1.4 % of KOs).
- **Levers, in order:**
  1. Arena geometry / void height: more ground between players and the void, or a softer edge.
  2. Bots that play safer near edges.
  3. Only then knockback growth.
- These are design / world changes, not cost changes, so they were not applied.

**Still flagged (not applied):**
- **Gun 3.5× and Staff 2.9× median** (already ×2 in pass 1). Held ranged weapons are inflated by bot aim; human data should decide further increases.
- **Cannon 2.9× and Bow 2.4×:** borderline.
- **Wings / Parachute / Jetpack / Cape / Ball:** low by design (utility).
## Balance pass 1 (previous build, kept for history)

_Hand-written summary of the generated sections below. Re-running the tournament regenerates everything except this section, so copy it forward._

**Applied** (cost only, in `assets/tuning/combat.ron`): `willpower.form_cost` sets **Gun ×2.0** and **Staff ×2.0**. The multiplier prices both the cast and the upkeep, because a held weapon's damage is sustained. Integrity is still priced from the unadjusted cost, so a pricier gun is not a tougher gun. Same 500 seeds before and after:
- **Gun:** 6.1× → 3.4× median damage per willpower.
- **Staff:** 5.2× → 2.8×.
- **Hand cannon:** 3.2× → 2.0× the median of its own archetype family.
- **Shoots:** 4.5× → 2.8×.
- **Global KO rate, KO %, fight time and win-rate spread:** unchanged.

Held ranged weapons are still above 2×. That is expected: bots aim with near-perfect lead, and humans won't. Stop here until human playtest telemetry (`logs/matches`) confirms it.

**Proposed, not applied** (not clear-cut):
- **`Hammer+Flies` (the flying hammer):** 11× median damage per willpower. It is an autonomous hunter priced as a held item (12 base, against 25 for a creature). Plain hammers are near the median, so a Hammer-wide cost increase would hit the wrong thing. Proposal: a surcharge when Flies / Homing / Charge appears on a held form. That would need a small new cost knob, so it is a design call.
- **Flies:** win rate 18–19% over ~560 appearances per run, against a 12.5% baseline. The flying hammer is most of this. It stays high after the Gun/Staff change, which makes it the next candidate.
- **Cannon (2.8×) and Bow (2.1×):** borderline. Re-check with human data.
- **Wings / Parachute / Jetpack / Cape and structures:** these deal ~0 damage by design (movement and defence). The "low" flags on them are not bugs.

**Smash targets.** Knockback is not the lever:
- **Blast zones:** 99% of KOs are bottom (void). Side and top are about 1% combined.
- **Credit:** only 54% of KOs are credited to an opponent.
  - 33% are bots dying without ever being hit since spawning (walking or falling off).
  - 13% had a hit, but a median 11 s before the fall. That is a real self-fall, not the 6 s `ko_credit_secs` window expiring.
- **KO %** on credited KOs: median 61. Target 110–170.
- **kb_growth sweep** (100 matches each, credited KO % median):

  | kb_growth | kb_base | KO % median |
  |---|---|---|
  | 4.0 | 1.0 | 62 |
  | 2.5 | 1.0 | 65 |
  | 2.0 | 1.0 | 68 |
  | 2.5 | 0.8 | 76 |

  Fight time rose 10.9 s → 12.7 s. Halving knockback moves KO % by only ~10, so the Smash knobs were left at their defaults.
- **Where KO % comes from:** how easily bots drop off the islands. The next levers are bot edge-safety (`bots.rs`) and arena / void design, not knockback.
- **Fight time** (first hit → credited KO): median 10.5 s, inside the 8–15 s target.
- **Recovery:** 75–79% of launches end in play, against a ~40% target. Launches rarely carry players past the side or top zones.
