# Balance report

_Generated by `cargo run -p bd_sim --release --example tournament` — 500 matches × 8 bots, 240 s cap / first to 10 KOs, seeds 1..500, 50% fuzz recipes + 50% phrase pool. Before = `default`, after = `assets/tuning/combat.ron`. Hidden data (mystery tenet): never shown to players._

## Findings and applied changes (balance pass 1)

_Hand-written summary of the generated sections below. Re-running the tournament regenerates everything except this section, so copy it forward._

**Applied** (cost only, in `assets/tuning/combat.ron`): `willpower.form_cost` sets **Gun ×2.0** and **Staff ×2.0**. The multiplier prices both the cast and the upkeep, because a held weapon's damage is sustained. Integrity is still priced from the unadjusted cost, so a pricier gun is not a tougher gun. Same 500 seeds before and after:
- **Gun:** 6.1× → 3.4× median damage per willpower.
- **Staff:** 5.2× → 2.8×.
- **Hand cannon:** 3.2× → 2.0× the median of its own archetype family.
- **Shoots:** 4.5× → 2.8×.
- **Global KO rate, KO %, fight time and win-rate spread:** unchanged.

Held ranged weapons are still above 2×. That is expected: bots aim with near-perfect lead, and humans won't. Stop here until human playtest telemetry (`logs/matches`) confirms it.

**Proposed, not applied** (not clear-cut):
- **`Hammer+Flies` (the flying hammer):** 11× median damage per willpower. It is an autonomous hunter priced as a held item (12 base, against 25 for a creature). Plain hammers are near the median, so a Hammer-wide cost increase would hit the wrong thing. Proposal: a surcharge when Flies / Homing / Charge appears on a held form. That would need a small new cost knob, so it is a design call.
- **Flies:** win rate 18–19% over ~560 appearances per run, against a 12.5% baseline. The flying hammer is most of this. It stays high after the Gun/Staff change, which makes it the next candidate.
- **Cannon (2.8×) and Bow (2.1×):** borderline. Re-check with human data.
- **Wings / Parachute / Jetpack / Cape and structures:** these deal ~0 damage by design (movement and defence). The "low" flags on them are not bugs.

**Smash targets.** Knockback is not the lever:
- **Blast zones:** 99% of KOs are bottom (void). Side and top are about 1% combined.
- **Credit:** only 54% of KOs are credited to an opponent.
  - 33% are bots dying without ever being hit since spawning (walking or falling off).
  - 13% had a hit, but a median 11 s before the fall. That is a real self-fall, not the 6 s `ko_credit_secs` window expiring.
- **KO %** on credited KOs: median 61. Target 110–170.
- **kb_growth sweep** (100 matches each, credited KO % median):

  | kb_growth | kb_base | KO % median |
  |---|---|---|
  | 4.0 | 1.0 | 62 |
  | 2.5 | 1.0 | 65 |
  | 2.0 | 1.0 | 68 |
  | 2.5 | 0.8 | 76 |

  Fight time rose 10.9 s → 12.7 s. Halving knockback moves KO % by only ~10, so the Smash knobs were left at their defaults.
- **Where KO % comes from:** how easily bots drop off the islands. The next levers are bot edge-safety (`bots.rs`) and arena / void design, not knockback.
- **Fight time** (first hit → credited KO): median 10.5 s, inside the 8–15 s target.
- **Recovery:** 75–79% of launches end in play, against a ~40% target. Launches rarely carry players past the side or top zones.

**Hidden** (mystery tenet): none of this data is shown to players.
